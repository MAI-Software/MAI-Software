## The idea

Generic planning tools are not built for people who create content with ADHD: they are either an endless list or a calendar that punishes whatever did not get done. MAI-Focus starts from the opposite place.

## How it works

Work is organised across **three horizons**: goals for the year, campaigns for the month and, day to day, **time blocks** you drag and resize on the grid, in steps of 5 to 30 minutes.

- **No red, no "failed" state.** Whatever did not happen gets rescheduled, not flagged as a mistake.
- **Labels with colour and shape.** Each category pairs one of 12 colours with one of 9 shapes, so it stays recognisable without relying on colour.
- **Calm mode** for overstimulated days, and breaks built into the plan.
- **Voice.** You can say "move the recording to 18:30" and it is understood on the device itself, showing what it understood before applying it.

## Privacy

Your data stays on the device: no mandatory account and no server, with backup export and import.

## Status

In development and usable in the browser. An Android version is planned.
