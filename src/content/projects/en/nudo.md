## The idea

Everyone has fought a pair of headphones that came out of a pocket in a knot. Nudo turns that moment into a puzzle: the rope lies tangled on the table, and the challenge is to leave it straight.

## How it plays

You drag the rope with the mouse and each section follows the one before, the way a real cable would. The trick is in the crossings: with **right click or Shift** the section you are moving passes **under** instead of over, which is what lets you undo loops that would otherwise only tighten.

While playing, the screen only shows what matters: a cable **integrity** meter, **points** and **time**. Each level starts from an everyday object — the first one, a pair of headphones.

## Status

In development and playable in the browser, with nothing to install.
