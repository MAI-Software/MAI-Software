## The brief

A tool for the worst moment of a working life: you are dismissed, the company hands you a figure, and you have no way to tell whether it is right.

## The problem

The employer has an accountant; the worker has nobody. And there is a basic confusion that costs money: **severance pay and dismissal compensation are not the same thing**. Severance is money already earned and always owed; compensation only applies when leaving wasn't your decision. Plenty of people sign for less because they assume one covers the other.

## The solution

From four inputs it returns the full breakdown: days worked, untaken holiday, the accrued share of extra payments, and compensation. It handles contracts predating the 2012 reform — calculated at 45 days per year rather than 33 — and flags which portion is exempt from income tax.

Most usefully: enter the amount the company is offering and it tells you **how much is missing**.

Calculation happens in the browser; data never leaves the device. The design departs from the rest of the family: dark background and amber accent, more sober for a delicate moment.

## Outcome

Live at elfiniquito.com, free and without sign-up, using 2026 regulations.
