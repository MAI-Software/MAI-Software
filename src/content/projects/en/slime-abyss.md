## The idea

A 3D platformer whose hero is not a solid character but a **liquid slime** made of many small blobs. You descend an abyss floor by floor until you reach the treasure.

## How it plays

The slime moves like a liquid: it stretches around curves, sheds droplets at corners and can **split** when it passes saws and **merge back** together. Your health is the slime itself: every drop that falls into the void or evaporates in fire is life lost.

Each floor introduces **one new mechanic**, first on its own and safely, then combined with the earlier ones: fire, switches, platforms, oil that burns, slippery ice, fans, freezing cold air, rails the slime rolls along like a ball, and loops that end up making it dizzy.

Around that there are coins and stars per floor, achievements that unlock traits to customise the slime's face, a collection of items and a level editor.

## Technology

The liquid physics are custom: particles held together by cohesion and a mesh rebuilt every frame. All models and textures are generated from Blender, and the sound is synthesised with an underwater cave ambience. It is available in five languages.

## Status

In active development and free to play in the browser. The goal is to release it on Google Play.
