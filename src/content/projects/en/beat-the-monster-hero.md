## Context

A turn-based JRPG where combat isn't played with buttons: the phone camera tracks the player's movement and turns punches into beats.

## Problem / opportunity

Touch controls limit the physical feel of a rhythm fighting game. The opportunity: use the camera as the controller and make the body the input system.

## Solution

Real-time motion detection, tempo-locked combos, gear progression and a coach that guides the player. Built for one hand and short sessions.

## Outcome / status

In active development. Playable web build available; Android APK in preparation.
