## The brief

When you look at a flat, the question isn't what it costs: it's **how much you'll pay every month and how much you need saved to start at all**. Bank simulators answer the first and dodge the second.

## The problem

Almost nobody reaches the end of the process with the sums done. The deposit isn't just 20% of the price: add tax, notary, land registry, conveyancing and valuation, and those costs change from one Spanish region to the next. That is exactly the part that catches first-time buyers out.

## The solution

A calculator that answers both at once. On one side the monthly payment, the interest over the life of the loan and a **year-by-year amortisation table** on the French system. On the other, purchase costs broken down, with each region's taxes.

Around it sit guides, an explanation of the method and a FAQ, because a figure with no context doesn't help anyone decide.

Everything is calculated in the browser: no sign-up, and the data never leaves the device.

## Outcome

Published and in use, with the rates and Euribor in force in 2026.
