## The brief

Sending a quote shouldn't eat half a morning. Anyone working for themselves ends up fighting a spreadsheet template, exporting to PDF and getting the tax wrong somewhere along the way.

## The problem

The alternatives ask you to create an account, put your tax details on someone else's server and, almost always, pay a subscription for something used a few times a month.

## The solution

One form and that's it: your details, the client's, the line items with quantity and price, and the taxes (VAT, income-tax withholding and discount). You can upload a logo, set the number, date and validity, **preview the document before generating it** and download it as a PDF.

None of it leaves the device: the maths and the PDF itself are built in the browser, with no account and no server involved. It ships in five languages, each on its own address.

## Outcome

Published, free and in use.
