## The brief

An app for not missing daily medication, built for the people who actually need it: older users and long-term treatments.

## The problem

Health apps ask for an account, upload your medical data to a server and fill the screen with charts nobody knows how to read. They also fail exactly when there's no signal.

And there's a barrier before all that: if the interface is complicated, the people who need it most won't use it.

## The solution

The home screen shows **one thing**: the next dose, large, with its time and a photo of the box. One tap to confirm, and just as easy to undo if you get it wrong.

Separately you can log blood sugar and blood pressure, with the latest reading always visible and a line showing whether it's rising or falling. The app **interprets nothing**: it records what you confirm so you can show it to your doctor. That restraint is a design decision, not a limitation.

Everything lives on the device: medication, schedules and photos. No account, no cloud, no trace once uninstalled. Reminders are fired by the phone itself, so they work without internet, and the lock screen won't show the medication name unless you ask it to.

## Outcome

Published for Android as a direct download. An iOS version is on the way.
