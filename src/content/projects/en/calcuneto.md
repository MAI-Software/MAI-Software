## The brief

A public tool answering the question everyone asks when signing a contract: *out of this gross figure, how much do I actually take home each month?*

## The problem

Existing calculators either asked you to sign up or returned a single number with no explanation. Nobody could see how much goes to income tax, how much to social security, or what that salary really costs the employer.

## The solution

A calculator that updates as you type and shows the full split: what you keep, income tax withheld, social security contributions and the total employer cost. It handles the variables that actually change the result — 12 or 14 payments, contract type, age, dependent children — and compares scenarios so you can negotiate with numbers.

Every calculation runs in the browser: nothing is sent or stored. Around it sit guides and an FAQ explaining each concept, which also makes the site findable in search.

## Outcome

Live at calcuneto.com, free and without sign-up, using 2026 tax figures.
