## The idea

Working out what is worth watching right now means opening four tabs: Twitch on one, YouTube on another, Kick, and television somewhere else. Simple question, scattered answer.

## The problem

Each platform only shows its own, ordered to suit itself. Finding a stream outside the channels you already follow comes down to luck, and comparing across platforms means hopping between them.

## The solution

One screen with whatever is live on Twitch, YouTube, Kick and Spanish television, viewer counts included. Around it sit trends, active creators, category filters (gaming, sport, music, esports) and the TV schedule.

MAI Live Stream **hosts and restreams nothing**: it gathers what is out there and sends you to each stream on its own platform, where the creator gets credit for the view.

No account needed.

## Outcome

Published and free, in Spanish and English.
